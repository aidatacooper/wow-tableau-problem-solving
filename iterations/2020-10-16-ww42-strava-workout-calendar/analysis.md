# 2020 Week 42: Strava training calendar

Article publication: 2020-10-16. Official challenge publication: 2020-10-13, WordPress post 4029. Challenge identity: 2020 Week 42. Official source: https://www.workout-wednesday.com/2020w42/ . The official publication date is Tuesday; the Wednesday date in the author's workbook filename is not the challenge publication date.

The author packages two separate extracts: a one-row-per-day calendar covering 2014 through 2021 and activity records with Activity ID, timestamp, miles and seconds. The builder preserves two independent native datasources. It never joins, merges or preaggregates the datasets into one input. Calendar is primary and activity data secondary. A date-valued BLEND: Date links calendar Date to DATE(DateTime). Multiple activities on the same day aggregate once; ZN returns zero for calendar days without matching activity.

The dashboard is 1680x1020 and has exactly three business sheets. Calendar shows a four-column by three-row month grid, with day-of-month positions and black daily-hours bars. A separate text pane within the same worksheet displays the month and WINDOW_SUM of blended daily hours at day28, partitioned by month. Weekly Profile plots native week-of-year blended hours in blue #0070A0. Three BAN panes display selected-year total hours, miles and number of activities. Rows/Cols calculations are dimensions; their labels are hidden. Calendar pane shading is #F5F5FA and white month separators remain distinct from the worksheet background. Bars have no stroke.

The YEAR(BLEND: Date) filter is shared by all three sheets. The original slider control hides the slider track and All choice, retaining year stepping. Three on-select True-to-False self-filter actions remove highlight. The verifier inspects native source/target/event and field bindings; it does not claim browser click or hover execution. REST states select2020 default,2019 and2018, with complete Calendar,Weekly Profile and BANs CSV exports. Daily completeness, zero days,12 monthly summaries, weekly sums and all3 whole-year BANs require independent raw-data verification.

Released SDK eb1380d reproduced two generic layout defects: show_slider=False was ignored, and a YEAR(field) dashboard filter dependency tried to find the entire expression as a physical column, omitting the year dependency. These require generic SDK fixes and synthetic date-filter regression tests; the case does not use private SDK members or direct XML edits to bypass them. Candidate enhancement provenance is kept separate from the released baseline and must be pinned to the final actual SDK commit.

The original workbook is analyzed only to recover data and semantics. The builder starts with TWBEditor(""). A comparison/export copy removes only hidden worksheet-window flags, preserving all business XML and packaged data, as recorded in export-provenance.json. The reference faithfully follows Donna's original layout: Weekly Profile is upper left and the title plus three footer text objects are retained. The official challenge's upper-right profile/one-text-object wording therefore is not claimed as a literal layout match.

Initial Cloud capture exposed real failures: a bare REST filter key silently retained the default year, and Calendar exported no rows despite populated Weekly Profile and BANs. The final request uses the independently tested lowercase year(BLEND: Date) filter for 2019 and 2018. Public incremental diagnostics isolated the conditional label Date grain and table-calculation partitioning. Calendar Text now has the same exact daily Date detail as Bar. Addressing Cols/Rows produced the annual total in every month and was rejected. The equivalent business calculation instead addresses Day, exact Date and the two conditional label dimensions while leaving monthly Rows/Cols partitioned. The existing public native dual-axis builder is used, with explicit fold=False axis styling to preserve separate upper text/lower bar regions. No original XML or private member is used in construction, and no claim is made that an automatic no-id pane alone caused the earlier empty queries.

Released-SDK synthetic evidence preserves the year-filter layout gap, typed column-instance addressing gap, and absence of a public multi-field rich-label method on 682f45. The final SDK e7fa7cd supplies generic configure_custom_label, retaining the existing table-calculation instance and resolving all rich fields through the public registry. Final acceptance is based on the actual Git installation, successful raw/native verifier, six paired Cloud images and all 18 complete business CSV exports across three genuine year states. See evidence/visual-review.md for the frozen full artifact hash, exact CSV coverage and remaining differences. No physical-phone, browser click, hover or tooltip interaction is claimed.
