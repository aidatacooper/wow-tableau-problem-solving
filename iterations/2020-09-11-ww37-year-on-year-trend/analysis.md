# Year-over-year subscription trend

The article is dated 2020-09-11. Official WordPress post 3978 identifies 2020 week 37 and its publication is 2020-09-08, despite the original workbook name saying September 9. Only article_date and challenge_date are business identity dates.

The untouched Hyper has 486,872 subscription records. Today is fixed at 2020-07-15, not the runtime current date. Subscription dates are normalized to year 2020. The context date filter initially includes January 1 through July 14, and Include Dates <Today is also a context filter. Those filters affect global MIN/MAX date LODs before the daily/weekly decision. CY=2020 and PY=2019 subscriptions are compared within that normalized window. The state map colors by (CY-PY)/PY, while black circles size by CY; circle tooltip share is CY divided by the window sum over displayed states.

A window with end-start <=30 days displays daily lines. Longer windows use weekly lines anchored to the weekday of the selected minimum date. Both Map and Trend discard all marks in the week containing the maximum date, using Date To Plot < Max Date Week; the verifier preserves this exact boundary even when it discards an apparently complete final week.

The filled map and black-circle panes include a native viz-in-tooltip embedding Trend, 500x400, with State membership passed to an action-filter group. This establishes the source, target, fields and artifact contract. REST directly exports Trend and does not claim to have triggered a hover.

Only Map, Trend and the custom subscription legend are rebuilt; the original CHK sheets and Viz construction experiments are analysis aids, not business dashboard requirements. The Map CSV and complete State/year/plotted-date Trend CSV cover all substantive dashboard metrics. Raw Hyper is independently aggregated by actual date and state, then compared against four REST states: default, California, a June date subset, and a May-June date subset. The default complete-week totals are CY 185,341 and PY 165,191; California is CY 19,528 and PY 17,600.

The two date subsets enumerate ISO dates as standard REST view-filter equality lists. Actual source and replica exports show that these are additional post-context filters: the native January 1-July 14 context remains unchanged. They therefore remain Weekly, Wednesday-aligned, including partial plotted weeks where the equality list removes days. They do not demonstrate changing the native date range or executing the daily/weekly switch. The verifier independently checks Daily June 1-21 and Monday-aligned May 4-June 14 calculations from raw records and validates the native context, LOD and switching formulas. Runtime range switching and actual tooltip hover remain outside the REST acceptance scope. Replica diagnostic MIN/MAX columns must stay January 1/July 14 in all captured states; every State/year/plotted-date mark and both map panes' CY/PY/YoY/share are checked against the post-context oracle. Documentation: https://help.tableau.com/current/api/rest_api/en-us/REST/rest_api_concepts_filtering_and_sorting.htm ; native URL range limitation https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=001458313&language=en_US&type=1 .

The public calculated Date alias supplies the visible date-control caption while preserving Baseline Date lineage and the initial context bounds. The replica applies Full Weeks only to Map as well as Trend, matching the source. Black subscriber circles and the custom legend follow the source size and caption.

The original comparison copy only exposes hidden worksheet windows; formulas, filters, data and layout are unchanged. The builder starts TWBEditor("") and only reads the extracted locked Hyper with public SDK APIs. No browser clicks or hover are executed.
