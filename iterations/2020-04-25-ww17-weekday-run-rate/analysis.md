# Weekday run rate against monthly plan

The dashboard helps compare month-to-date sales, projected weekday run rate, and monthly plan across four regions. The official challenge is 2020 WW17, published on 2020-04-21; the article is dated 2020-04-25. These are the only business dates in metadata.

The extracted actuals contain 92 daily region records and the plan extract has four monthly region records. The source business date is the maximum actual date, 2020-04-23. The independent oracle enumerates calendar weekdays rather than copying the Tableau calculation: April has 22 weekdays, of which 17 have elapsed. Run rate equals month-to-date sales divided by 17 and multiplied by 22. Central 9,537 becomes 12,342 against plan 13,000; East 3,703 becomes 4,792.117647 against 4,500; South 6,482 becomes 8,388.470588 against 14,000; West 10,288 becomes 13,313.882353 against 6,500.

Actuals and Plan remain separate Hyper datasources blended on region and month. A join would duplicate monthly plan by the number of actual dates. Viz is a single three-pane worksheet: a compact regional text panel, a horizontal run-rate bar, and a black monthly-plan Gantt marker folded over that bar. Under-plan run rate is red in both text and bar; the other values use gray or black. Tooltips are disabled. The fixed phone-sized dashboard is 375 by 667, with title and four regional rows. Data is an off-dashboard validation worksheet; its complete four-region CSV exports MTD, Run Rate, and Plan, not merely a control or title sheet.
