# WW46: latest-year sales differences by category and segment

## Business question and acceptance
For every product category and customer segment, compare sales in the latest year present in the data with the immediately preceding calendar year. The difference is current minus previous, not an absolute value or a percent. The Gantt mark starts at previous sales and ends at current sales; losses extend in the negative direction. Zero uses the loss classification. Null prior-year totals remain null rather than becoming fabricated zeroes.

Sources: Donna Coles article https://donnacoles.home.blog/2021/11/18/can-you-recreate-this-difference-chart/ and official challenge https://www.workout-wednesday.com/2021w46/ . Intake confirmed canonical wow-2021-ww46-can-you-recreate-this-difference-chart, article 2021-11-18, official challenge 2021-11-16. Official page retrieved successfully on continuation after an earlier HTTP 502. Its requirements are an 800x400 dashboard, one sheet, no more than seven calculations, matching chart/spacing and no hardcoded dates.

Article analysis establishes: global latest order year (FIXED MAX), previous year minus one, conditional sales for each year, SUM(CY)-SUM(PY), zero-or-negative boolean, and a duplicated PY measure for the Gantt axis. Category is on columns, Segment/Measure Names on rows. PY and CY bars precede the Gantt difference, then a blank row. Both layers share year-aware tooltips; title years are dynamic. No user filter or dashboard action is requested. The Measure Names filter is an internal chart-construction filter, not a user control.
